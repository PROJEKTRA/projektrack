# Demo script 0.9 (20 minutes)

From a booked hour to an issued invoice, with synthetic data only. Labels are the English ones.

## Prepare (before the audience arrives)

1. Use a test installation that is not reachable from the internet (`docs/admin/plain-hosting.md`): `APP_ENV=staging`, `MAIL_MAILER=log`. Never use a real mail server: no mail must leave the machine.
2. Create a new, empty company. Country BA, currency BAM (the defaults). Use invented values:

   ```
   php artisan projektrack:install --slug=demo --name="Primjer d.o.o." --admin-email=admin@example.test --admin-name="Demo Admin"
   ```

3. Fill it:

   ```
   php artisan projektrack:demo demo
   ```

   Result: 3 customers, 5 projects, 8 people, 3 months of approved time, one service report per project, 2 draft invoices. It refuses a company that already has customers, and `APP_ENV=production`.
4. Run `php artisan schedule:run`. Open the setup link from the newest file in `storage/logs/`. Set the password, sign in and turn on two-factor sign-in.
5. Open **Users** > **Invite**: `Demo Gost`, `gost@example.test`, role **Employee**. Run `php artisan schedule:run` again and set the password in a second browser profile. Sample people cannot sign in.
6. As administrator, open the project **Savjetovanje**. Under **Team** add `Demo Gost` as **Member**.
7. Open **Hourly rates** > **New rate**. **Kind**: **Cost rate**. **Applies to**: **Company default**. 60 BAM, from the first day of this month.
8. Open **Settings** > **Invoices**. Enter an invented legal name, street, postal code, city, tax number, VAT ID, bank and IBAN. Leave **Legal notices confirmed by an accountant** off.
9. Go through the script once on a throwaway copy. Then set up a fresh one: issued invoices cannot be removed.

## Script

| Minute | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Sign in with the second factor. **Dashboard**. | Everything here is sample data. Nothing leaves this machine. |
| 2 to 4 | **Customers**: a customer with tax profile and contact. **Projects**: **Health** and work packages of **Savjetovanje**. | Customer, project and budget in one place. |
| 4 to 8 | Second browser, `Demo Gost`: **Timesheet**. Type `1:30` today in a work package and a description. Start the timer, then **Stop and book**. **Submit week**. | A week takes a minute. Submitted time is locked. |
| 8 to 10 | Administrator: **Approve time**. Select, **Approve**. | Nobody approves their own time. Approved time never changes; corrections are entries of their own. |
| 10 to 12 | **Hourly rates**: company default and two personal rates. **Contribution margin**: period this month. | Value and cost are frozen at approval. The sample months have no cost rate: they show as missing, not as zero. |
| 12 to 14 | **Service reports**: **New report**, **Savjetovanje**, last month, **Generate**. Download **PDF** and **XLSX**. | Only approved time. A report never changes. |
| 14 to 17 | **Invoices**: open a draft. Show the lines and the tax. **Issue invoice**, confirm with the password, then **Issue invoice** again. Open the **PDF**. | The number has no gap. An issued invoice never changes. |
| 17 to 18 | On this invoice use **Credit note** with a part of one line. | Wrong amounts are corrected with a document of their own. |
| 18 to 19 | Profile: change the language to Bosnian or German. **Languages**: **Edit texts**. | Own words for your company. |
| 19 to 20 | **System check** and the limits below. | Say what is not finished. |

## Say it plainly

- The legal notices need confirmation by an accountant. Show only the domestic invoice, which has none.
- E-invoices are not checked with KoSIT or veraPDF. Do not present them as valid.
- Runs of the business modules on MySQL, MariaDB and PostgreSQL, with Docker and on Apache are still open.
- Delivery is staged (decision C-06). Content and dates: `docs/release-notes/0.9/en.md`.
- Quotes, absences, expenses and the web installer are not built yet.

## If something goes wrong

- **Before the invoice can be issued:** lists what is missing. Most often it is a field from step 8.
- No setup link: run `php artisan schedule:run` again and check the newest log file.
- "This tenant has customers already": use a new, empty company.
- **System check** reports the missing HTTPS and `APP_ENV`. That is right for a test machine.
- A work package does not appear for `Demo Gost`: check step 6.

## Afterwards

Delete the test installation with its database and logs. The logs hold sign-in links.
