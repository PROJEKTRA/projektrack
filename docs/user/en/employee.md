# Quick start: employee

Book your week and submit it. You need an account and a place in the team of a project.

## Set your password

1. Open the link in the mail with the subject "Reset your password". It is valid for 60 minutes.
2. Enter a long password twice and choose **Reset password**.
3. Choose **Log in** with your email address and password.
4. Link expired? Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and request a new one.

## Turn on two-factor sign-in

1. Open the menu with your name, then **Settings** > **Security**.
2. Confirm with your password if you are asked.
3. Choose **Enable 2FA**.
4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or copy the setup key. Choose **Continue**.
5. Enter the 6-digit code and choose **Confirm**.
6. Save the **2FA recovery codes** in a password manager. They are shown only once.

## Book your week

1. Open **Timesheet**. Each row is a work package, each column a day.
2. Row missing? Choose it under **Add a work package**. The field appears if you have many packages.
3. Click a cell and type the time: `1:30`, `1,5` or `1.5` are all one hour and a half.
4. Press Tab or an arrow key. Each cell is saved at once.
5. Type what you did in the field **Description** below the table. Your company can require it.
6. Wrong value? Choose **Undo** to take back the last change, or empty the cell.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Arrow up, arrow down | Previous or next row |
| Enter | Next row |
| Arrow left, arrow right | Previous or next day, from the start or end of the text |
| Tab | Next field |

A red total marks a long day. A day never takes more than 24 hours. A locked month takes no time.

## Use the timer

1. Choose a **Work package** above the table, then **Start timer**.
2. Add a **Description** when you are done and choose **Stop and book**. Whole minutes are added to the cell of the day.
3. Over midnight, each day gets its part.
4. Started by mistake? Choose **Discard**. Nothing is booked.

## Submit

1. Check the week. Use **Previous week**, **This week** and **Next week** to move.
2. Choose **Submit week**.
3. Submitted and approved time is shown in grey below the cell. You can no longer change it.

## Returned entries

1. You get a notification with the reason. The cell has a red border.
2. Click the cell. Below the table you see **Returned:** and the reason.
3. Correct the time or the **Description**.
4. Choose **Submit week** again.

Approved time wrong? Tell your controller, who books a correction.
