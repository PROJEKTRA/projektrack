# Quick start: project lead

Set up a project, approve time and create a service report. Sign-in: see the employee quick start.

## Create a project

1. Open **Projects** and choose **New project**.
2. Enter the **Name** and choose the **Customer**. Customer missing? Ask your controller.
3. Choose the **Billing model**. Only **Time and material** and **Mixed** can be invoiced from time.
4. Fill in **Start**, **End** and **Budget (hours)** if you know them.
5. Set **Warning from (%)**: from this share of the budget the project shows **Budget nearly used**.
6. Check the **Currency** and choose **Save**. You are now the lead of the project.

Contracts are entered by the controller. Ask for one before you assign it to work packages.

## Plan work packages

1. Open the project. Under **Work packages** choose **New work package**.
2. Enter the **Name**. Use **Part of** to place it under another package.
3. Choose the **Contract** and **Service category** if they apply.
4. Enter **Planned hours**. Clear **Billable** for work you do not charge.
5. Choose **Save**.
6. Arrange packages with the arrows: **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move under the package above**, **Move one level up**.
7. Done with a package? Choose **Edit work package**, set **Status** to **Closed** and choose **Save**.

## Build the team

1. Under **Team** choose the **Person** and the **Project role**.
2. Choose **Member** for people who book time. Choose **Add**.
3. Someone leaves? Choose **Remove** next to the name.

People need an account first. Your administrator invites them.

## Approve time

1. Open **Approve time**. It lists submitted time in your projects, never your own.
2. Select entries, or use the box in the header (**Select all**).
3. All in order? Choose **Approve**.
4. Something wrong? Select the entries, type a **Reason** and choose **Return**. The person gets a notification.

Approved time never changes. Your controller books corrections.

## Create a service report

1. Open **Service reports** and choose **New report**.
2. Choose the **Project** and the period with **From** and **To**. The last month is suggested.
3. Choose **Generate**. The report contains only approved time.
4. A message tells you if entries of the period are not approved. Approve them and generate a new report.
5. Download it as **PDF**, **XLSX** or **CSV**.

A report never changes. The option **With amounts** is for the controller.

## Watch the budget

1. Open **Projects** and look at **Health**: **Good**, **Budget nearly used** or **Over budget**.
2. Open a project to see the booked hours per work package.
